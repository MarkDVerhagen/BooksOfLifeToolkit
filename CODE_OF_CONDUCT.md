# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using
welcoming and inclusive language, being respectful of differing viewpoints,
gracefully accepting constructive criticism, and showing empathy toward other
community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct
which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
